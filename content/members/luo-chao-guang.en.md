## Research overview

Chaoguang Luo holds a PhD in Management from Sichuan University, with undergraduate and master's training in Computer Science and Technology. His work focuses on AI-driven management decision-making, organised around two main lines — recommender systems and user behaviour modelling, and online review mining and consumer analytics — combining machine learning, graph and hypergraph learning, text mining, and configurational/causal analysis. His publications appear in journals including Tourism Management, Information Processing & Management, Applied Soft Computing, and Neurocomputing.

## Research interests

Recommender systems; online review mining; graph and hypergraph learning; data mining and causal analysis.
